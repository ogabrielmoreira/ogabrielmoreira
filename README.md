## 👋 About

6+ years shipping digital products across design and engineering (9+ years in tech overall). My work sits at the intersection of **Design Systems**, **component architecture**, and **AI-augmented workflows**. Background includes Natura &Co, Banco BRB, Bradesco, and Toyota do Brasil, including a **144% ROI** outcome leading Natura's multi-brand design system (GaYa), where I built 200+ tokens and 150+ components with a Figma-to-code architecture.

## 🛠️ How I work

I bridge design and code: building scalable component libraries, enforcing WCAG 2.2 accessibility standards, and integrating LLMs into development workflows for code review, documentation generation, and quality automation. AI is a daily tool I use to close the Figma-to-production gap, not a shortcut. I design, prototype, and ship. IBM-certified in Generative AI Engineering.

## 🚀 Currently

- Running **Gabriel Tech Design** (freelance/MEI): websites, product design, and AI-assisted development for clients.
- Building out a portfolio with case studies including **GaYa** (Natura &Co design system), **OTTO Vigilant** (automotive HMI/AI concept), and **Lumen** (personal design system).
- Open to **Design Engineer / Senior Product Designer** roles: remote, international, ideally compensated in USD.
